# 18. VERIFICATION SYSTEM

Every content record must have one of:

VERIFIED
- Supported directly by official government/INEC/National Assembly record.

REPORTED
- Supported by credible news reporting but not independently verified by an official primary document.

SELF-REPORTED
- Published by Senator Aduda or his official organization.

PENDING
- Insufficient evidence; do not display as a factual claim.
