# Sen. Philip Aduda: sourced projects and photos (research notes, September 2026)

These notes cover what public sources say about Sen. Philip Aduda's service and constituency projects across the six Area Councils of the FCT. Every item links to its source. The same information is loaded into the platform with `python -m app.seed --aduda`: it appears on **Our Record** marked *Pending review*, and on his profile page.

> **Before you present:** confirm figures (costs, road lengths, dates) with his office. Some reports disagree; for example, the Sabon Gari road is reported as both 4 km and 6 km.

## Photos

I could not download photos: this build environment blocks the news websites. The photos in these articles also belong to the newspapers or photographers, so reusing them without permission risks copyright complaints and takedowns. The best route:

1. **Ask his media team for official photos.** This is the best option: high quality, and cleared for use. Ask for:
   - an official portrait for the profile page;
   - project photos, ideally before and after, for each road, hall and centre above;
   - commissioning-ceremony photos, such as with the Senate President.
2. **His official accounts** have many project photos. Download them with his team's agreement:
   - X: [@SenatorAduda](https://x.com/SenatorAduda)
   - Instagram: [@officialphilipaduda](https://www.instagram.com/officialphilipaduda/)
3. **Take new photos** of the finished projects: Nyanya road, Sabon Gari road, Karu town hall, Shadadi road, and the Jikwoyi/Nyanya sports centres. NIPAM then owns them outright.
4. **News photos** (Leadership, Tribune, Vanguard, Daily Trust, ThisDay) can be linked, but should only be reused with the publisher's permission, credited to them.
